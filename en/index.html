01The project

A house carried on several shoulders.

Since 2012 the former manor house has belonged to the Berlin architect and urban planner Tim Lehmann, who has been renovating it step by step ever since. About half of the living space is home to the Meteoriten-WG, a shared household that for most of its members is a weekend country project. The other half, mainly the ground floor, is let on a permanent basis. Some residents have lived in the house for fifty years, and that is how it should stay.

The house is now being divided into freehold flats, and responsibility for house and garden will be shared among several owners. Nine flats are being created in the main house, one of them a community flat in the former dining hall. Two garden houses on the grounds have already been sold. The three flats on the upper floor, WE 6, WE 7 and WE 8, are now for sale.

The project has the character of a building group, because not everything is finished yet, and of a cooperative, because much is used in common: sauna, garden, outbuildings, the dining hall. Legally it is conventional freehold ownership (Wohnungseigentum) with its own land register sheet. For a small community that is the simplest arrangement and offers the greatest security.

Status

Planning permission for the division has been in place since July 2025, and the division is currently under way. Handover is expected in 2028. Until then there is time to get to know each other, plan and prepare. Anyone who likes gardening can get involved earlier.

The project is evolving continuously; subject to change.

02The flats

Three flats on the upper floor, plus 76 m² shared.

All three flats are on the first floor of the Villa Meteor. Each comes with a quarter of the common areas on that floor: library, fireplace room and two balconies, one of them with a view of Lake Ziernsee. The flats are sold in their current state. About eighty per cent of the renovation is done; the rest can be completed and designed to your own ideas within the scope of the planning permission.

Budget

Beyond the purchase price, a budget for fitting out should be planned. For WE 8 the owner reckons with a total of 230,000 to 250,000 euros over the coming years, depending on how the flat is designed and what else is done to the house and garden. Central heating is currently being installed; energy efficiency class still F for now.

03House & community

A bit of a building group, a bit of a cooperative.

Whoever joins buys a flat and shares the rest: the sauna, the roughly 4,000 m² grounds with gardens and a play meadow, workshop and tools, wood store and fire bowl, the covered bicycle shed. The former dining hall of the estate, today the shared kitchen, is designated in the declaration of division as a community flat, for small events, workshops or the occasional pop-up café. If you like cooking and want to open a restaurant for a day: be our guest.

Also planned are an outdoor kitchen and a garden bath, shared Wi-Fi on the grounds (Kleinmenow has fibre broadband), a fitness and yoga room in the basement and shared use of the boat and bathing jetty in agreement with the jetty association. A car can be shared with the neighbours.

You should explicitly be looking for a community and bring experience of one, and be up for a little casting: the four co-owners already on board want to form their own impression too.

04Location

At the end of the world, one train hour from Berlin.

Kleinmenow lies in the west end of Fürstenberg, at the end of an avenue of robinias, in a terminal-moraine landscape of wide forests and lakes. Lake Ziernsee is 200 metres away, with a line of sight from the house; three bathing spots are in the immediate vicinity, plus clear-water lakes such as the Peetschsee and the Stechlin. With 28 inhabitants per square kilometre, Fürstenberg is as sparsely populated as Sweden.

Fürstenberg (Havel) station is four kilometres away, a quarter of an hour by bike on a pretty route. From there the RE 5 regional express runs hourly without a change to Berlin, to Gesundbrunnen, Hauptbahnhof, Potsdamer Platz and Südkreuz, and in the other direction to the Baltic Sea. A car of your own is not strictly necessary. Fürstenberg has supermarkets, restaurants, cafés, a DIY store and a pretty old town.

05History

From leasehold farm to fairy-tale castle.

  1. 1418

    Menow is first mentioned in a document.

  2. 1765

    The Klein Menow farm becomes an independent dairy farm; in 1810 it is raised to a hereditary leasehold farm.

  3. 1862

    In January a bucket-sized meteorite strikes Kleinmenow, one of only 46 documented falls on the territory of the Federal Republic of Germany. Its fragments can be seen in museums worldwide today. A memorial stone at the eastern tip of the estate commemorates it and the shepherd who, with his flock, got away with a fright. The meteorite “Meno” gives the villa its name.

  4. c. 1900

    The tenant’s residence is built, in many details indebted to late historicism and the Heimatstil. In front of the house an old lime tree stands on the roundel; to the east a landscaped park with a tea house adjoins; to the west, towards the lake, lies the farmyard with stable, barn, greenhouse, smithy and pump house. A windmill pumps water into the reservoir in the attic.

  5. 1920s

    General Paul Fleck, already retired, is the owner of the estate.

  6. 1930s

    The wooden balcony above the vine-covered veranda is replaced by a generous terrace on brick columns with sculptures.

  7. 1945

    The owner Bernhard Kirchner is expropriated by the Soviet army in the course of the land reform.

  8. GDR

    The façade ornaments are removed and the house divided into six rented flats. In the village it is known as the “fairy-tale castle”. The Stölke and Kruggel families move in and live here to this day.

  9. 1998

    The terrace is demolished because of neglected maintenance.

  10. 2012

    The municipal housing company of Fürstenberg sells the house to the Berlin architect and urban planner Tim Lehmann. Since then it has been renovated step by step; the balcony is rebuilt as a timber structure modelled on the original. On the 150th anniversary of the meteorite fall, the Meteoriten-WG moves in.

  11. 2023

    Permission for two garden houses in place of outbuildings.

  12. 2025

    In July, planning permission is granted for dividing the main house into flats.

  13. Today

    The house is shared by long-established residents and newcomers to Fürstenberg, and used by Berliners as a weekend retreat.

More on the history of the house: Gutshaus Kleinmenow on gutshaeuser.de, the directory of manor houses (in German).

06Quick questions

What most people want to know first.

Can you live in Kleinmenow permanently, or only at weekends?
Both. Some residents have lived in the house for decades, others use it from Berlin as a weekend retreat. A main residence and a second home are both possible.
How do you get to Kleinmenow without a car?
Fürstenberg (Havel) station is four kilometres away, a quarter of an hour by bike. From there the RE 5 runs hourly without a change to Berlin and, in the other direction, to the Baltic Sea.
Can you work from here?
The common areas on the upper floor include a fireplace room with a coworking space. Kleinmenow has fibre broadband, and shared Wi-Fi on the grounds is planned. Berlin is one train hour away, so home office and workation are realistic.
What is shared?
The sauna, the roughly 4,000 m² grounds with gardens and a play meadow, workshop and tools, wood store and fire bowl, the covered bicycle shed and the former dining hall as a community flat. Shared use of the boat and bathing jetty is planned in agreement with the jetty association.
Is this a cooperative or a building group?
Legally it is conventional freehold ownership with its own land register sheet, a homeowners’ association under German law. In character the project has something of a building group, because not everything is finished yet, and of a cooperative, because much is used in common.
Are dogs allowed?
Yes, dogs are allowed.
Is there an agent’s commission?
No. The sale is commission-free and direct from the owner.
